# Amplop specs

These specs turn the Amplop PRD into units of work that can be built, tested and committed one at a time. Each spec says what to build, which PRD rules it serves, the exact interfaces it uses, and how to prove it is done.

## How to use them

1. Read `01-conventions.md` first. It fixes every shared name: contract functions and events, API endpoints, database tables, ids, environment variables, money formats.
2. Pick a spec whose dependencies are done. Build it with its tests. Tick its acceptance checklist.
3. When a spec is unclear, the order of authority is: the PRD, then `01-conventions.md`, then the spec. A spec that fills a gap in the PRD says so under "Decisions made here".
4. Commit as PRD 15 says: one finished piece per commit, `type(area): what changed`, pushed at least daily.

The PRD is the "Amplop PRD" doc; `PRD.md` here is a snapshot of it from 10 Oct 2026 (revision 73). If the doc changes, re-export it. `REVIEW-NOTES.md` records how these specs were checked against the PRD. The mock-ups are the "Amplop app mock-ups" design; a copy of every board's HTML is in `design/boards/`, the mock-up design rules in `design/SPEC.md`, and the logo files in `design/logo/`. Where mock-up copy and the PRD differ (for example "Ibu" in older boards), the PRD wins: the parent is "Mama".

## Build order

The PRD schedule (section 16) sets the days. In spec terms:

1. 02, 03, 04 (contract and gates first; a contract fix after the app is wired costs a redeploy).
2. 05 and 06 together, then 07 and 10 (two linked phones reading live balances).
3. 09 and 15's Skip time and View as.
4. 08 and 12 (the whole video script without the indexer).
5. 16, then 11 and 14, then 13.
6. 17, 18, 19's hand-run tiers, then 20.

If a day slips, cut in the PRD's order (section 16): recovery screens in 13, lock-off and trusted-person replacement in 13 and 10, moving purchases in 11, Midtrans in 08 (keep Simulate payment), alerts in 14, Activity in 11 and 16. Never cut what PRD 16 marks "never cut".
