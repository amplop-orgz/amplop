# Interview summary

Spec 20 S20-10. One page, about 600 words or fewer. **To fill by the builder from the real interviews only.** Nothing here may be invented: if fewer than 10 people were interviewed, say the real number. No names and no contact details; quotes only with permission, attributed by role ("a student in the Netherlands", "a parent in Surabaya").

## Who and how

**To fill:** how many students and how many parents; where they study or live (city or country only); when; how (call, chat, in person).

## What students said

**To fill:** the three or four points that came up most, each with how many said it.

## What parents said

**To fill:** the same, for parents.

## What changed because of it

**To fill:** the design decisions the interviews caused (for example weekly release, or an Emergency approval that cannot block).

## Quotes

**To fill:** two to four short quotes, with permission, by role only.
