## Constructor and roles

The constructor takes the token, the settlement address, the treasury, the card service and the demo switch. It reads the token's decimals once. There is no owner, admin, pause or upgrade: the deployer keeps no power after deployment. Roles are fixed: the **owner** of an account (the student's passkey address), its **trusted person**, the **treasury** and the **card service**.

## Events

Every write emits exactly one event, with `accountId` indexed wherever it applies: `AccountCreated`, `SplitSaved`, `InviteCreated`, `TrustedLinked`, `TrustedRemovalStarted`, `TrustedRemoved`, `Deposited`, `PurchaseApproved`, `PurchaseDeclined`, `PurchaseCollected`, `HoldReleased`, `PurchaseRefunded`, `PurchaseMoved`, `EmergencyRequested`, `EmergencyOpened`, `EmergencyCancelled`, `LockOffStarted`, `LockOffDone`, `RecoveryApproved`, `RecoveryDone`, `PendingCancelled`, `DaysSkipped`.

## Always true

These six statements hold after every call, and invariant tests check them with random sequences of calls:

1. The contract's AUSD balance equals all the money it accounts for.
2. Locked Emergency money leaves only through R17 to R20.
3. A purchase lowers exactly one envelope.
4. Collected money goes only to the settlement address.
5. Only the allowed caller can make each call.
6. Every wait reads the account clock.

## Weeks and the account clock

Days are counted from the Unix epoch on the account clock. A week starts on Monday at 00:00 UTC; a share is released when the account clock passes that Monday. Balances are computed on read from a few counters and at most three live release records, so no weekly transaction is needed.

## Invite binding

An invite is accepted with an EIP-712 signature by the invite's one-time key over `AcceptInvite(accountId, trusted)`. The domain names chain 10143 and this contract's address, and each invite key works once per account, so a signature cannot be reused on another chain, another contract or another account.
