The examples use Raka, a student in Amsterdam, and Mama, his trusted person in Jakarta.

## For the student

### Create your account

Tap **Create a passkey**. Your phone asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock; there is no password and no recovery phrase. Then type your name and set your envelopes.

### Set your envelopes

Every allowance is split into five envelopes by percentage: Food, Transport, Study, Everyday and Emergency. The percentages are whole numbers in steps of 5 and add up to 100. Any cents left over from rounding go to Everyday (R4). The default is Food 40%, Transport 15%, Study 15%, Everyday 20% and Emergency 10%.

### Weekly shares

Food, Transport, Study and Everyday each open in four equal shares: the first when the allowance arrives, the next three on the following three Mondays at 00:00 UTC. Any leftover cents go into the last share (R5). Money you do not spend stays in its envelope and carries over (R6). The Emergency share arrives whole and locked (R7). A second allowance gets its own four shares, and each envelope shows the sum of everything released so far (R8).

The home screen shows what is available this week, a bar with a dark line for where you should be today, and the next release date. Tap an envelope for its release schedule and this week's spending.

### Which envelope pays

The kind of shop picks the envelope (R9):

| Envelope  | Shops                                                 |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Food      | groceries, restaurants, fast food and canteens        |
| Transport | public transport, taxis and ride-hailing, fuel        |
| Study     | stationery, printing and copying, schools and courses |
| Everyday  | bookshops, cinemas, and every other kind of shop      |
| Never     | gambling                                              |

If the matching envelope has enough, it pays. If not, and Everyday has enough for the whole amount, Everyday pays. Otherwise the purchase is declined; a purchase is never split across envelopes (R10). Emergency never pays a shop directly (R11). Gambling is always declined, no money moves, and your trusted person sees only the category (R12).

### Holds and collection

An approved purchase is first held in its envelope. The shop collects up to the held amount within 7 days; if it does not, the hold goes back to the same envelope. A refund returns to the envelope that paid (R13). Prices in euros are turned into dollars at a sample rate when the purchase is approved (R14).

### Move a purchase

A purchase paid from Everyday can be moved to Food, Transport or Study, once, within 7 days, if that envelope has enough. Everyday gets the amount back, and that kind of shop uses the new envelope from then on (R15). Open the purchase in **Activity** and choose **Move to another envelope**.

### Ask for Emergency money

Open **Emergency**, enter an amount up to the locked balance and write a reason (up to 280 characters). One request can be open at a time, and you can cancel it (R16). Your trusted person reads the reason and can approve, which opens it at once; they cannot refuse. If nobody approves, it opens 48 hours after you asked (R17). Opened money moves to Everyday and can be spent straight away; the rest stays locked (R18). With no trusted person, the request simply waits 48 hours (R19).

### Settings

- **Envelope split.** A new split takes effect 48 hours after you save it, and only for allowances that arrive after that. Money already in envelopes is not re-divided. Before your first allowance, a new split applies at once (R21).
- **Emergency lock.** Switching it off takes 48 hours. After that the whole Emergency balance moves to Everyday and later Emergency shares arrive unlocked (R20).
- **Trusted person.** An invite link works once and expires after 7 days. The first trusted person is linked at once; replacing or removing one takes 48 hours (R22).
- **Recovery.** If you lose your passkey, open **Lost your phone? Recover your account** on a new phone, create a new passkey and send the recovery link to your trusted person. After they approve there is a 48-hour wait, then the account moves to the new passkey (R23).
- **Cancel a change.** Every waiting change (R20 to R23) shows a countdown and a Cancel button until its wait ends (R24).

## For the parent

### Accept the invite

Open the link Raka sent on WhatsApp and tap **Accept and create a passkey**. That makes your own passkey; no password.

### Send an allowance

On your home screen tap **Send allowance**, enter an amount in rupiah between Rp100.000 and Rp50.000.000, and continue. You get a BCA virtual account number to pay from your banking app. The amount in dollars uses the sample rate shown on the screen ($1 = Rp16.500), and the fee is Rp0. Once the payment arrives, the money is split into Raka's envelopes and you see Allowance sent with its transaction proof.

### What you see

For each envelope you see the total left: what is available plus what is not released yet. You never see a shop or a list of purchases (R25). Alerts appear in the app when Emergency is requested, a blocked purchase is declined (category only), an envelope falls under 20% of this week's share, a purchase is moved (envelope names only), a rule change is saved, or a recovery is approved (R26).

### Approve Emergency

When Raka asks, the request appears first on your home screen. Tap it to read the reason and tap **Approve with passkey**. You can open it sooner, but you cannot block it; it opens by itself after 48 hours.

### Approve a recovery

If Raka loses his phone, he sends you a recovery link. Only approve if you have spoken to him yourself: whoever holds the new passkey will control his envelopes. After you approve there is a 48-hour wait, and Raka can cancel it from the old phone if it was not him.
